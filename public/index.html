About

Every point is kept on this device and sent to your team server when there is signal. Coordinates are WGS84 (EPSG:4326); UTM is shown and exported alongside. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics; OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA).
